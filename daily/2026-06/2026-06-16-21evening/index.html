Moussouris 5 大核心论断 — "Banning Anthropic's advanced models is going to hurt defenders more than attackers"

来源：The Register 6/15 21:00 | Luta Security 博客 | TechCrunch 6/15 19:00 | heise 6/15
论断 ① "没有真正的 jailbreak"：
"There was no guardrail bypass or jailbreak. Defenders should be able to ask AI systems to find and fix bugs, and write tests to validate the patch."
论断 ② "模型在做合法防御工作"：
"Anthropic's models were doing 'the most valuable thing an AI model can do for defensive security: executing the find, fix, and test loop defenders run every day.'"
论断 ③ "撤市反噬"：
"Removing the capability for models to respond to defensive requests makes AI systems 'worse at finding bugs and verifying patches.'"
论断 ④ "出口管制无效"：
  • 美国无法对开源权重系统或中国等地类似先进模型扩展出口管制
  • 这些系统不久将达到 Mythos-like 能力
  • Anthropic 和 Google 已指控中国对手（包括 DeepSeek）使用 "distillation attacks" 训练其模型
论断 ⑤ "战略风险"：
"Banning Anthropic's advanced models is going to hurt defenders more than attackers."
"Defense improves when defenders find the same bugs attackers find and fix them faster. We need the best tools to defend against increasingly capable attackers in the AI era of cybersecurity."
"To pull the best capabilities away from defenders without a good reason when our adversaries are rapidly advancing is dangerous."
