U.S. legal jurisdiction over a U.S.-based cloud provider can expose European data even when it is stored in Europe." + "Even if data resides in Europe, a U.S.-based provider may still be subject to U.S. legal demands, including the CLOUD Act, which allows American authorities to compel disclosure from U.S. companies regardless of where the data is stored." + "Sovereignty, then, is not about where the server rack sits. It is about whether the operator, the keys, the audit trail, and the disclosure process are actually under the control of the institution that claims ownership."
