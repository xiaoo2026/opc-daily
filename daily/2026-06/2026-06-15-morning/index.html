核心问题：
"It frames everything as an argument between you and it, gives caveats about things you didn't say, and raises beside-the-point semantic nits all over the place. Never, ever does it use the word 'technically'. Everything is a confrontation."
5 原因分析：
  1. Excess Alignment Guardrails：assumes every user input is an attempt to get it to do something bad → "ironically this has resulted in an extremely misaligned chatbot"
  2. 出口管制粗制 guardrails：Fable 被加 guardrails 应对监管 → "Deeply misguided, likely unconstitutional, ineffective" / "Turning one specific frontier model into an asshole for all users isn't fixing the problem"
  3. Anti-Sycophancy Training 失败：simply prompting to be "less agreeable" → 变得粗鲁 / "I'd like to gently push back" = 被动攻击
  4. 训练数据可能是 Reddit 过度拟合：treats everything as flame war
  5. 最可能：coding 训练主导导致 chat 退化：there are no headline metrics for chat but there are for coding, and all the money is in coding / "Sonnet 4.6 best for human conversation, worst for technical/coding"

Fulcrum Research 6/9 Inverse Rubric Optimization 论文——HN #48485277 21pt/0c "agent science" 首次实证框架

来源：Uzay Girit / Eleni Shor / Kaivalya Hariharan / Rowan Huang | Fulcrum Research 6/9 | HN #48485277 21pt/0c | fulcrum.inc/2026/06/09
研究目标：
"Studying long-horizon agents is challenging due to high trajectory-level variance, costly evaluations, and the very settings that produce rich behavior also being the noisiest. Fulcrum Research seeks general principles of agent performance across settings — a nascent 'agent science' — through toy settings that remain challenging, rich, and smooth."
实验配置：
  • 任务：诗歌生成（5 rubrics × 5 topics）
  • Generator：Always Haiku 4.5
  • Optimizers：Opus 4.6 / GPT-5.5 / Fable 5 / Haiku 4.5
  • Judge：Opus 4.6
  • Seeds：3 per optimizer × 5 judges
5 个关键发现：
  1. Performance is Smooth in Label Budget — Mean final score improves consistently with label budget
  2. Models Employ Rich Strategies — iterate, test hypotheses, notice patterns / Fable 和 Opus 系统化程度高于 GPT-5.5 / Fable 5 在 Milton 评测中 budget 1000 关闭 91% 差距到 rubric-visible policy
  3. Models Do NOT Maximally Use Their Budget — Fable 5 never uses more than 1000 labels when given a budget of 10000, which explains its plateau at 10000, whereas Opus 4.6 keeps improving from 1000 to 10000 / "natural effort scale" 概念首次实证
  4. Different Approaches to Resource Use — GPT-5.5 快速偏向更大 batch sizes / Anthropic models 渐进增加 batch size
  5. Fable 5 Plateaus — Fable 5 outperforms all models at smaller label budgets but does not improve at the largest budget and plateaus around the level of Opus 4.6
