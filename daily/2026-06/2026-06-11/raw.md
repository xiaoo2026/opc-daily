### 🟢 事件 A：Microsoft 6/10 内部禁用 Fable 5——Anthropic 30 天留存与 Microsoft ZDR 首次公然冲突

**事件核心**（The Verge 6/10 首报 + mlq.ai 6/10 + AI Weekly 6/10 + Windows Forum 6/10 + Techmeme 6/10 同步）：

**Microsoft 6/10 公告核心**：
- **Microsoft 内部 GitHub Copilot model picker 移除 Claude Fable 5**（6/10 当日）
- **早期 Claude 模型（Opus 4.8 / Sonnet 4.6 / Haiku 4.5）仍可内部使用**（保留 Zero Data Retention）
- **外部 GitHub Copilot / Microsoft Foundry 客户仍可使用 Fable 5**（retention policy opt-in + 默认 disabled）
- **冲突根源**：Microsoft 内部 ZDR compliance 标准 vs Anthropic 30 天必选留存

**Anthropic 6/10 公告完整规则**：
- **Mythos-class 模型所有提示词与输出** = **30 天留存**（用于信任与安全审查）
- **content flagged for policy violations** = **可保留 2 年**（用于法律调查）
- **ZDR 用户**：必须为整个 workspace 关闭（不只是 Fable 5）

**Microsoft 内部 ZDR 与 Anthropic 30 天的具体冲突**：
- **Microsoft 内部合规标准**：员工使用的所有 AI 工具**不能留存 prompt/output**
- **Anthropic 6/10 新规**：Fable 5 / Mythos 5 **必选 30 天留存**（即使是 ZDR enterprise 用户）
- **冲突点**：Microsoft 员工发到 Fable 5 的代码 / 业务数据会**默认** 30 天存储在 Anthropic
- **Microsoft 决策**：**内部禁用**（"Microsoft 卖 + 不用"分裂）

**The Verge 6/10 关键引语**（mlq.ai 引语）：
> "Microsoft is restricting employees from using Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 because of Anthropic's new 30-day data retention requirements. The conflict centers on a new Anthropic policy specific to Mythos-class models: all prompts and outputs are retained for 30 days by Anthropic's safety classifiers, and content flagged for policy violations can be stored for up to two years."

**Windows Forum 6/10 深度解读**：
> "Microsoft is selling access to the model while hesitating to let its own employees use it, and that contradiction says more about the AI market than any benchmark chart could."

> "The more revealing story is that **enterprise AI is entering a phase where model capability, safety architecture, and data governance are no longer separable buying criteria**."

**GitHub Changelog 6/10 同步**（AI Weekly 引用）：
> "Fable 5 is the only model in Copilot's Claude lineup that breaks Zero Data Retention because Anthropic's safety classifiers require retaining prompts and outputs for up to 30 days and flagged items for up to two years. For external GitHub Copilot customers, the policy is opt-in and disabled by default; **enterprise admins must explicitly enable it, making Microsoft's employee restriction a function of its own internal compliance posture rather than a blanket product limitation**."
